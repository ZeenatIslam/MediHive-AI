# MediHive AI · Agentic Healthcare Triage & Patient Management Platform · Built with Gemini AI

MediHive AI is an AI-powered healthcare operations platform that helps hospitals and clinics automate patient triage, prioritize emergency cases, manage queues, and assist doctors with intelligent decision-making.

Patients describe their symptoms, and MediHive AI analyzes severity, recommends the appropriate department, identifies emergencies, prioritizes the queue, and supports healthcare professionals with actionable insights.

Humans provide medical care; AI handles the operational intelligence.

## What the platform does

Healthcare facilities often struggle with patient overcrowding, manual triage, and delayed emergency identification. MediHive AI replaces traditional first-level screening with an intelligent agent layer.

| Agent               | Role                                                                         | Gemini Capability          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Symptom Analysis    | Converts patient symptoms into structured medical data                       | Structured Output          |
| Triage Agent        | Determines severity, urgency level, suggests department, and assigns doctors | Reasoning & Classification |
| Department Routing  | Recommends the appropriate medical department                                | Multi-Step Decision Making |
| Emergency Detection | Identifies potentially critical cases                                        | Risk Assessment            |
| Queue Management    | Assigns patient priority automatically                                       | Intelligent Prioritization |
| Doctor Assistant    | Generates patient summaries and clinical recommendations                     | Context-Aware AI           |
| Forecasting Agent   | Predicts patient inflow, department workload, and resource demand            | Predictive Analytics       |
| Revenue Agent       | Analyzes treatment revenue, billing trends, and operational efficiency       | Business Intelligence      |
| Analytics Agent     | Generates hospital performance insights and reports                          | Data Analysis & Reporting  |
